Nextly

Your personal AI job partner.

Nextly is a Norwegian AI-powered job-search platform that brings the entire process into one place — from finding relevant positions to landing the next job.

It is built for job seekers in Norway, including people facing redundancy and EU/EEA citizens moving to Norway for work.

  • 01Job listings gathered from sources such as NAV and EURES
  • 02Tailored CVs and applications for each position
  • 03AI-assisted interview preparation

Restoq

Plan. Allocate. Execute.

Restoq is a web-based inventory and purchasing platform for bars and restaurants, giving managers and staff one shared tool for stock, ordering and supplier invoices.

Managers set up the system and approve invoices, while staff handle stock counts, orders and goods receipts.

  • 01Tracks stock levels, shelf life and waste
  • 02Suggests supplier orders based on sales and stock
  • 03Matches invoices against orders and deliveries before export to Tripletex

Start my company

Start a company in the right country — with AI as your guide.

Start my company helps entrepreneurs choose the right country and company type for a new business, compares costs and taxes, and prepares the documents needed to get started.

It covers nine countries: Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Estonia, Poland, Switzerland, the UAE, the Philippines and the United Kingdom.

  • 01Guided AI interview about the business, owners, tax, banking and visa needs
  • 02Comparison of nine countries on start-up cost, company tax, local requirements and time
  • 03Formation documents, articles of association and shareholder register for a Norwegian AS, plus checklists for every country
