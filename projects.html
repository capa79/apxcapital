Nextly

Your personal AI job partner.

Nextly is a Norwegian AI-powered job-search platform that brings the entire process into one place — from finding relevant positions to landing the next job.

It is built for job seekers in Norway, including people facing redundancy and EU/EEA citizens moving to Norway for work.

  • 01Job listings gathered from sources such as NAV and EURES
  • 02Tailored CVs and applications for each position
  • 03AI-assisted interview preparation

Restoq

Plan. Allocate. Execute.

Restoq is a web-based inventory and purchasing platform for bars and restaurants, giving managers and staff one shared tool for stock, ordering, write-offs and supplier invoices.

Supplier invoices are read with AI and checked against orders and goods receipts before approval. The app is available in English, Norwegian, German, Polish and Spanish, and can carry each venue's own logo and colours.

  • 01Suggests supplier orders from sales, stock, open orders and shelf life
  • 02Reads supplier invoices and flags price and quantity deviations before they are sent to accounting
  • 03Logs write-offs by reason and shows what is thrown away most — and what can be done

Start my company

Start a company in the right country — with AI as your guide.

Start my company helps entrepreneurs choose the right country and company type for a new business, compares costs and taxes, and prepares the documents needed to get started.

It covers nine countries: Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Estonia, Poland, Switzerland, the UAE, the Philippines and the United Kingdom.

  • 01Guided AI interview about the business, owners, tax, banking and visa needs
  • 02Comparison of nine countries on start-up cost, company tax, local requirements and time
  • 03Formation documents, articles of association and shareholder register for a Norwegian AS, plus checklists for every country

Nordkurs Trading

Own-account currency operations, fully documented.

Nordkurs Trading is an internal platform for own-account currency operations in NOK, EUR and PLN. It brings exchange positions, bank accounts and supporting documentation together in one place.

The tool is used internally only. It is not offered to customers, and no currency services are provided to third parties.

  • 01Overview of currency positions and exchange history across NOK, EUR and PLN
  • 02Own bank accounts connected through open banking for automatic reconciliation
  • 03Documentation trail for every transaction — receipts, customs declarations and KYC/AML records
