How we handle information when you visit apxcapital.no.

Who is responsible

APX Capital is responsible for the processing described here. Questions about privacy can be sent to post@apxcapital.com.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits and see which pages and links are used. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store information on your device. We see aggregated figures such as page views, referring websites, browser, operating system, screen size and country.

To tell visits apart, GoatCounter briefly combines your IP address and browser information in memory. According to GoatCounter, IP addresses and browser identifiers are never written to its database or disk.

We also count clicks on a few links, such as the links to our ventures and our email address, to understand what visitors are interested in.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use the statistics to identify individual visitors, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Email

If you contact us by email, we use the information you send to answer your enquiry and keep the correspondence for as long as needed for that purpose.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of and deletion of personal data about you, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Contact us at post@apxcapital.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
